Fullcale › Baby tracker

Baby tracker & growth diary,
in your calendar

A baby tracker app, a family calendar, a photo album… the number of apps jumps the day your baby arrives. Fullcale keeps them in one place.

Baby log

Log feeding, diapers, sleep, weight and temperature with a tap. Fullcale reminds you when the next feeding is due, so you're not trying to remember the last one at 3am.

Growth diary

Save milestones — first roll-over, first crawl — with photos. Each entry shows your baby's age automatically, so you'll always know when it happened.

  • Up to 20 photos per entry
  • Free: 5 photos per day (10 after watching an ad). Pro: 50 per day
  • Filter to “milestones only” to see the big moments
  • Add more than one child

With your family's calendar

Put checkups and vaccinations on the calendar, and share photos on a shared family calendar. With a partner on the same shared calendar, you both know what's coming.

FAQ

Is the baby tracker free?

Yes. Growth diary photos are 5 per day on the free plan (10 after watching an ad) and 50 per day with Pro.

Can I track more than one child?

Yes. Add each child and keep separate records.
