A calendar app with
a built-in budget tracker

Downloaded a budget app, used it for three days, forgot it existed? Putting the budget inside the calendar you already open every day changes that.

Why budget apps don't stick

Most budgeting apps fail for a simple reason: you have to remember to open them. On a busy week, you don't — and once you've missed a few days, catching up feels like too much work.

In Fullcale, the budget lives inside your calendar. You're already opening it to check today's plans, so logging what you spent takes a few seconds.

What the budget tracker does

Log by voice

On the Budget screen, tap the mic and say “Lunch 15 dollars”, “Uber 20 dollars” or “Groceries 60 dollars”. Fullcale picks the category and the amount. Typing works too.

See where your money goes

  • This month's income, expenses and balance, with a comparison to last month
  • Spending ranked by category
  • Daily and monthly trends, average spending per day
  • Your five biggest expenses

Recurring payments, logged automatically

Add subscriptions, rent or insurance once as recurring payments, and they're logged on their due date every time.

A calendar view of your spending

See how much you spent on each day of the month, right next to what you were doing.

The budget is private to you. It is never shared with shared-calendar members.

FAQ

Is the budget tracker free?

Yes, it's included in the free plan.

Can I share my budget with my partner?

No. The budget is private by design and is not shared through shared calendars.

Which currency does it use?

You can choose the currency symbol shown in the app. Amounts are recorded as whole numbers.
