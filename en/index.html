Typing events is a chore

Just say it. It's scheduled.

Tap the mic and talk the way you normally would. Fullcale picks out the date, time and title. No time mentioned? It becomes an all-day event.

Dentist tomorrow from 3pm to 4pm→ Tomorrow 15:00–16:00
Every Monday gym→ Weekly repeating event
Delete tomorrow's meeting→ Removes the event (undo available)
Keeping the family in sync

Share plans — and photos.

Create a shared calendar and send an invite code. Members get a push notification when someone adds an event, and every day can hold shared photos and a one-line memory from each member. Works across iPhone and Android.

When is my next period?

Period tracking, right on your calendar.

Log your period and Fullcale shows your predicted next period and PMS window on the same calendar as your plans. Your period log is never shared with shared-calendar members.

Predictions are estimates only and are not medical advice.

Baby days fly by

Baby log & growth diary.

Track feeding, diapers, sleep, weight and temperature, get a reminder for the next feeding, and keep a photo growth diary that shows your baby's age automatically.

Editing events is fiddly

Let the AI Secretary handle it.

Type a request and the AI Secretary adds, edits or deletes events, tasks and budget entries for you. If more than one item matches, you pick from a list, and deletions always ask for confirmation.
