A period tracker
right on your calendar

Planning a trip or a big day and wondering if it lines up with your period? With Fullcale you don't need to switch apps to check.

Why put period tracking in your calendar?

When your period tracker and your schedule are separate apps, you end up flipping between them every time you plan something. Fullcale keeps both in one place, so you can see what's coming while you plan.

What you can do

  • Log your period and see your predicted next period on the calendar
  • See your estimated PMS window
  • Check how many days until your next period at a glance
  • Look back at past cycles

Privacy

Your period log is stored in your own account and is never shared with shared-calendar members.

Predictions are estimates based on the cycles you log. Cycles vary from person to person and month to month, so don't use them for medical decisions or as contraception. Talk to a healthcare professional if you have concerns.

FAQ

Is period tracking free?

Yes, it's included in the free plan.

Can people on my shared calendar see my period log?

No. Your period log is private to you.

How accurate are the predictions?

They're estimates based on your logged cycles. Don't use them for medical decisions.
