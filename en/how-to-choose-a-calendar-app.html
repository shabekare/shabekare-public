How to choose the best
calendar app for you

There are a lot of calendar apps, and “best” depends on how you'll use it. This guide helps you figure out what to look for.

This guide is written by the team behind Fullcale. It covers how to choose any calendar app, not just ours.

Start with what you need

The most common mistake is picking the most popular app and finding it doesn't fit how you live. Decide which of these matters most:

If you want to…Look for
Add events as fast as possibleVoice input or natural-language entry
Coordinate with family or a partnerShared calendars, notifications on changes, works on iPhone and Android
Manage work meetingsSync with your work account and other tools
Track more than eventsBudget, diary, period or baby tracking in the same app
Check plans without opening the appHome screen and lock screen widgets

Five things to check

1. How easy is it to add events?

A calendar only works if you keep putting things in it. If date and time pickers slow you down, choose an app that lets you add events by voice or by typing a sentence.

2. How does sharing work?

For family use, check how easy invites are, whether members are notified about new events, and whether it works across iPhone and Android. Being able to keep personal and shared events separate matters too.

3. What else do you want to track?

If your budget, diary, period and kids' records each live in a different app, you'll open fewer of them over time. One app that holds them all is easier to keep up with.

4. Readability and widgets

Can you read events in the month view? Can you color-code them? Is there a widget that shows today's plans on your home screen?

5. Price and ads

Check what's free and what the paid plan adds. If ads bother you, look at what it costs to remove them.

Which type of app suits you?

Built-in and big-name calendars

Apple Calendar and Google Calendar integrate tightly with your phone and accounts. They're a good fit if you need to sync with a work account or just want a simple list of events.

Sharing-focused calendars

If sharing with a family or group is the main goal, consider an app built around shared calendars and communication between members.

All-in-one calendars

If you want your budget, diary, period and baby tracking alongside your events, an all-in-one calendar app is the better fit.
